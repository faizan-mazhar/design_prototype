What the cameras watch for on site

Food sites have hygiene rules that vary by area. We agree each rule with your quality team before building.

  • Hygiene zone entry

    People moving from a low-care to a high-care area without following the agreed route or changing step, flagged to the supervisor.

  • Hairnets, beard snoods and coats

    Required coverings checked in the areas where they apply, with a still frame sent to the person who can act.

  • Hand-wash and sanitation steps

    Whether the agreed steps were done before entering a zone, with a short clip kept as evidence for audits.

  • Cleaning and changeover checks

    Whether a line was cleaned and set up in the agreed order after a changeover, logged for the quality record.

  • Foreign object and line faults

    Visible foreign items on a belt or a stopped line, raised for an operator who decides whether to halt production.

  • Cold room and door discipline

    Chilled and cold store doors left open, with the time they stayed open logged for the shift lead.

Rules your team sets and changes

You decide what the agent looks for, who is told and how quickly. Rules follow your own hygiene procedures and are written down before launch.

  • Hygiene zones and routesPersonYour quality team defines the zones, the allowed routes and the changing steps for each.
  • What each area requiresPersonCoverings and PPE are listed per area, because low-care and high-care rooms differ.
  • Who is told, and whenPersonRouting by line, area and shift, so the right supervisor sees the right alert.
  • Products stay with peoplePersonThe agent never holds, releases or rejects product. A named person makes that decision.
  • What counts as audit evidencePersonYour team decides which events and clips are kept for audits and for how long.

Privacy and people in a food plant

Production staff are on camera for much of the day. These points are agreed with them before a camera goes live.

  • Hygiene, not surveillanceRules look for coverings, steps and zones, not for who someone is. Alerts support hygiene, not performance scoring.
  • Video stays with youProcessing runs on your own servers or private cloud, so raw video is not sent to applyd.ai.
  • Changing areas and washroomsCameras do not belong in changing rooms or washrooms. We recommend views of entrances and production areas only.
  • Blurring, retention and assessmentBlurring and retention limits are set per camera, and we help with a data protection impact assessment and consultation with staff.
