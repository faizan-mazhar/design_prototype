Five agents, one job each

Five steps for every conversation, sales or service: capture, qualify, follow up, book and sync. Every stage has a written rule for when a person takes over.

Agents run on the platform your team controls. Read the security overview or book a demo to see it on your funnel.

Picks up leads from forms, site chat, shared inboxes and ad lead forms. Removes duplicates and enriches the record before anyone looks at it.

Hands to a person whenthe lead belongs to an open opportunity. It goes straight to that owner.

Your team decides where the agents stop

You set the hand-off rules before launch and can change them at any time. Agents never negotiate, discount or commit on your behalf unless you allow it.

  • Pricing and contract questionsPersonRouted to the account owner with the conversation so far.
  • High-fit accountsPersonAbove your score threshold, a rep takes the first call.
  • New message templatesPerson approvesNothing new is sent until a manager approves it.
  • Routine follow-up and schedulingAgentHandled end to end and logged in the CRM.

How does an AI agent hand a lead over to a sales rep?

Each of the five agents has a written rule for when it stops. When a lead meets a rule, such as asking about pricing or crossing your score threshold, the agent passes the conversation to the right rep with the full history and a short brief, and the CRM record shows who took over.
