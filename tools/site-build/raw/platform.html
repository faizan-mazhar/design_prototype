The AI agent platform behind applyd.ai: the controls sales and service leaders and security reviewers ask about first, and the numbers a pilot reports back.

One platform for sales and service teams

The agents run on a multi-tenant platform with the controls sales leaders and security reviewers ask about first.

Two-way CRM sync

Agents read account context before acting and write results back in real time. A rep's manual edit always wins.

Separate workspaces

Each business unit, region or brand gets its own data, playbooks and permissions.

One history per lead

Email, SMS, WhatsApp, chat and voice land in a single conversation, so a rep never asks the same question twice.

Agent versus human reporting

Compare response time and conversion for agent-handled and rep-handled leads, stage by stage.

How a website chat hands off

A scripted example of an agent answering from approved answers, asking two questions, and passing pricing to a person.

Quality you can see

You should not have to take the agents' word for it. Quality is measured the same way for agents and for your team.

Agent versus team metrics

Response time, meetings booked and hand-offs, side by side for leads the agents handled and leads your team handled.

Every action traced

Each message, score and CRM write is recorded with the rule that allowed it, so a manager can open any lead and see why.

Failures become test cases

When an agent gets a lead wrong, that lead is added to the evaluation set, so the same mistake is caught before the next change goes live.

What makes an AI agent platform ready for enterprise teams?

It keeps each business unit's data separate, syncs with the CRM in both directions without overwriting a rep's edit, holds one conversation history per lead, and records every agent action with the rule that allowed it. applyd.ai provides these, and reports agent and human results side by side.

What a pilot measures on your own leads

Every pilot runs agents on a share of your leads and compares them with your current process. These are the numbers we report back.

  • Time to first response

    Median minutes from lead creation to first reply, agent versus team.

  • Qualification rate

    Share of leads reaching your fit threshold, with the reasons logged.

  • Meetings held

    Meetings that took place, not just booked, per hundred leads.

  • CRM completeness

    Required fields filled on agent-worked records versus manual ones.
