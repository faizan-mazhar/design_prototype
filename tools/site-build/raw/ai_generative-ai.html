What we build with generative models

Most projects start with one valuable feature. We agree the quality bar and how we will test it before the first prompt.

  • Drafting and summaries

    First drafts and short summaries built from records already in your system, editable before anything is sent.

  • Document generation

    Reports and proposals assembled from your templates. The model fills the sections that need judgement and fixed clauses stay fixed.

  • Image generation and editing

    Visuals within style references and content filters, from resizing campaign assets to text-to-image drafts.

  • Reading images and audio

    Features that read photos and scanned forms or transcribe calls, feeding the same structured data.

  • Review queues

    Bulk generation that a person signs off, with approval screens and a history of what changed.

  • Brand voice and structured output

    Tone guides, banned phrases and examples steer the style, and schemas make every response fit your data.

How a generative pipeline is built

Good generated content comes from a pipeline, not a prompt box: structured inputs, grounding in your facts, automatic checks, and an editor in charge of what ships.

How we keep generated content safe

A fluent draft can still be wrong. The checks sit between the model and your customer.

  • Facts come from your recordsGroundedNames, figures and dates are inserted from your data, not recalled by the model.
  • Banned claims and drift checksCheckedEach draft is checked against your rules and the source facts before an editor sees it.
  • Editor approval where it countsPersonAnything that costs money or trust if wrong is approved by a person before it goes out.
  • Provenance on published contentLoggedEach item keeps its sources, checks and approver, and is labelled as AI-assisted where needed.
