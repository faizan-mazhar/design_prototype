Open days, applications and enrolment for schools and universities

An AI agent for schools, colleges and universities. It books open days, reminds applicants about deadlines and answers enrolment questions on WhatsApp, chat and email. Admissions decisions stay with your staff.

Book a demo

How students and parents reach admissions

  • WhatsApp and live chatStudents and parents ask about courses, dates and fees on the channel they already use, and get a prompt answer.
  • Email from applicants and parentsMessages are read, answered from approved wording and logged on the applicant's record.
  • Open day and event registration formsSign-ups are confirmed, reminded and followed up after the event.
  • Application portal promptsApplicants with an unfinished application are nudged before the deadline, with a link to continue.
  • Phone and voicemailMissed calls become records with a summary, so admissions can call back knowing the question.

From open day to enrolment, one step at a time

  • Book the open dayVisitors choose a date, get a confirmation and a reminder, and can move or cancel in one reply.
  • Remind applicants before deadlinesApplicants who have not finished get a short nudge on your schedule, with exactly what is missing.
  • Answer enrolment questionsFees, key dates, entry requirements and next steps are answered from wording admissions has approved.
  • Hand over warm applicantsWhen someone is ready to talk, an admissions officer gets the full conversation, so they are not asked to repeat themselves.

Rules for admissions decisions, safeguarding and young applicants

  • Admission decisions and offersPersonLeft to admissions staff. The agent does not say whether an applicant will get a place or what grades are needed beyond published requirements.

One applicant's journey, from open day to enrolment

What admissions and registry teams ask before a pilot

  • Can an AI agent support the applicant journey?

    Yes. It books open days, sends deadline reminders, answers enrolment questions and hands warm applicants to an admissions officer with the full history. Decisions on offers, places and scholarships stay with your staff. You choose the channels, the wording and the opening hours, and can change any of them at any time.

  • Can it work on WhatsApp?

    Yes. Many students and parents prefer WhatsApp, so the agent answers there as well as on chat and email, and all messages join one history. You choose the channels, the opening hours and the wording it uses. Admissions staff can step into any thread.

  • Does the agent make admissions decisions?

    No. It does not say whether someone will get a place, assess an application or promise an offer. It repeats published entry requirements and passes anything individual to admissions staff with the conversation attached. Your registrar decides the rules. Your registrar decides what the agent may say.

  • What about applicants under 18?

    Your rules cover this. You can set how the agent speaks to young people, when a parent or guardian is involved and which topics always go to a person. Any safeguarding concern is passed to your designated lead at once.

  • How do deadline reminders work?

    You set the schedule and the wording. The agent checks the application record, tells each person exactly what is still missing and stops once it is done. Applicants can reply to ask for a call from admissions at any point.
