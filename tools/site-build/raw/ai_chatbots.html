What we build for chat

Most projects start with one channel and one audience. We agree the scope, the hand-over rules and the test set before building.

  • Customer service assistants

    Order, account and policy questions answered from your help content and systems, each with a link to its source.

  • Internal knowledge assistants

    Staff ask in plain language and get an answer from your policies and wikis, limited to what they may already open.

  • Hand-over to people

    The agent who takes over sees the transcript, the customer's details and what the assistant already tried.

  • Several channels

    A website widget, a messaging app, Teams, Slack or a chat screen in your own app, all sharing the same knowledge and rules.

  • Languages and voice

    Replies in the language the customer writes in, and speech in and out where phone calls matter more than chat.

  • Analytics and guardrails

    A view of what people asked and where the assistant handed over. Unanswered questions become your content backlog.

How we keep assistants safe

An assistant speaks for your business. These limits are set before launch.

  • Approved content onlyGroundedAnswers come from sources you approve, and the assistant says when it does not know.
  • Hand-over rules you writePersonComplaints, money, sensitive topics and direct requests go to a person with the whole conversation.
  • Input and output guardsCheckedPersonal data is masked, injection attempts blocked and replies checked before they are sent.
  • A test set of real questionsTestedEvery release is run against questions your team actually gets.
