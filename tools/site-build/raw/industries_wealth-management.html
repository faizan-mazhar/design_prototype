Prospect qualifying and client reviews for wealth managers

An AI agent for wealth managers. It qualifies referred prospects, books review meetings with existing clients and prepares each adviser with the background. Advice and recommendations stay with your advisers.

Book a demo

Where prospects and clients start the conversation

  • Referrals from clients and professionalsIntroductions from clients, accountants and solicitors carry their source, so you can see which relationships bring good prospects.
  • Email to advisers and the officeMessages are read, sorted and answered in your tone, with the adviser copied on anything personal.
  • Review invitations to existing clientsThe agent invites clients to their annual review and offers slots from the adviser's calendar.
  • Website enquiriesVisitors who ask about working with you are asked a few approved questions and passed to the right adviser.
  • Phone and voicemailMissed calls become records with a summary, so an adviser can call back with context.

Qualify the prospect, arrange the review

  • Qualify against your criteriaAgents ask the questions you approved, such as the type of need and rough timing, and leave out detailed financial figures.
  • Book the first conversationProspects pick a slot from the right adviser's calendar, with a confirmation in your approved wording.
  • Arrange client reviewsExisting clients are invited, reminded and rebooked if needed, so reviews happen on schedule.
  • Prepare the adviserEach meeting comes with a short summary of the source, the questions asked and what the person wants to discuss.

Rules for advice, suitability and client confidentiality

Examples of rules a team in this field might write. You set the real ones, and can change them at any time.

  • Investment and pension advicePersonPassed to an adviser. The agent does not recommend products, say what to do or comment on markets.
  • Transfers, tax and suitability questionsPersonLeft to a qualified person. The agent notes the question and does not guess.
  • Fees and termsApprovalOnly fee information you have approved is shared, and anything bespoke is left to an adviser.
  • Bereavement or financial difficultyPersonPassed to a person at once, handled under your own policy.
  • Qualifying questions and meeting bookingAgentAsked in approved wording and booked on the adviser's calendar.

A referred prospect, from introduction to first meeting

What advisers and compliance leads ask about wealth clients

  • Can an AI agent qualify prospects for a wealth manager?

    Yes, lightly. It replies to referrals and enquiries, asks the questions you approved and books a first conversation with the right adviser. It does not ask for detailed financial figures or give any advice, which stays with your advisers. Your compliance lead approves the questions.

  • Can it arrange client review meetings?

    Yes. It invites existing clients to their review, offers slots from the adviser's calendar, sends reminders and rebooks when plans change. Each adviser gets a short note of past messages and topics the client wants to cover. You choose how often reviews are offered.

  • Does the agent give investment advice?

    No. It does not recommend products, comment on markets or say whether someone should transfer a pension. Those questions pass to a qualified adviser with the history attached. Your compliance lead decides what the agent may say, in line with your obligations.

  • Will it sound right to high-value clients?

    You write the tone and the approved wording, and advisers can review early conversations before the agent works on its own. Anything personal or sensitive is passed to a named adviser, so clients reach a real person whenever it matters.

  • How is client confidentiality protected?

    The agent collects only what booking and qualifying need, and asks people not to share account details by chat. You decide what is stored, and your data protection lead should review the setup. Our security overview explains how data is handled. We make no claim of certification.
