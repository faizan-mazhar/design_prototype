What the cameras watch for on site

Sites change week to week, so we start with the areas that matter most and agree the rules before building.

  • Hard hats and hi-vis

    PPE checked in the areas where it is required, with an alert and a still frame for the supervisor on that part of the site.

  • Exclusion zones

    People entering marked areas such as open excavations, lifting zones or edges, flagged while someone can still intervene.

  • Plant and people

    Excavators, dumpers and delivery vehicles moving near workers, logged as near-misses with a short clip.

  • Out-of-hours entry

    People or vehicles on site outside agreed hours, with a still frame for whoever is on call.

  • Gate and delivery logs

    Vehicle arrivals at the site entrance logged with time and a still frame, to compare against booked deliveries.

  • Progress snapshots

    Regular time-stamped views of agreed areas, so managers can look back at what was done and when.

Rules your team sets and changes

You decide what the agent looks for, who is told and how quickly. Rules are written down before launch and updated as the site changes.

  • Where each zone isPersonYour H&S lead marks exclusion and PPE zones on the camera view and moves them as work progresses.
  • Working and non-working hoursPersonThe times that count as out of hours are yours, with exceptions for planned late work.
  • Who is told, and whenPersonRouting by area and shift, with an on-call route for out-of-hours alerts.
  • Distance for plant near workersPersonSet per machine type, so the limit for a dumper can differ from the limit for a crane.
  • What a person must reviewPersonAnything affecting a worker is reviewed by a named person before any follow-up.

Privacy and people on a building site

Sites include employees, subcontractors, visitors and sometimes passers-by. These points are agreed before a camera goes live.

  • Cameras aimed at the siteWe recommend views that cover the work area and entrance, masking neighbouring property and public footpaths where possible.
  • Subcontractors and visitorsSignage at the entrance and induction briefings tell everyone cameras are in use and why.
  • Video stays with youProcessing runs on site equipment or in your private cloud, so raw video is not sent to applyd.ai.
  • Blurring, retention and assessmentBlurring and retention limits are set per camera, and we help with a data protection impact assessment and consultation.
