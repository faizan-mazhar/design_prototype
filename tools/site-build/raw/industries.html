One set of agents, your kind of work

The same five agents and the same written hand-off rules, doing the job that matters in each field: enquiries, bookings, orders, claims and renewals.

Why the work looks different by industry

A SaaS team gets demo requests and trial signups. A logistics team gets quote requests full of lanes and volumes. An online shop gets order questions, a clinic gets booking requests and an insurer gets claim reports. Some topics should never be answered by software. The first reply, the questions worth asking and the moment a person should step in all change with the business.

applyd.ai starts from the way your team already works. You choose the channels, the approved wording and the hand-off rules, and the agents follow them. The pages below show what that looks like in practice.

Customer and sales agents

  • SaaS

    Demo requests go cold within hours, and trial users who stall rarely hear from anyone.

  • Financial services

    Onboarding stalls on missing documents, and every chase email costs a team member time.

  • Insurance

    Claims happen at night and quotes are abandoned half-finished, and both need complete details to move.

  • Healthcare

    Empty slots and missed appointments cost a clinic time, and clinical questions must always reach a clinician.

  • Telecom

    Coverage questions are answered in seconds or not at all, and a customer who asks to cancel is easiest to keep in the first minute.

  • Logistics

    Quote requests arrive as loose emails missing the weight or the collection date, and customers keep asking where their load is.

  • Real estate

    Portal enquiries come in all evening, and negotiators waste viewings on people who cannot proceed.

  • Retail and e-commerce

    Shoppers ask about orders and products in messages, and slow replies lose the sale and fill the inbox.

  • Travel and hospitality

    Group enquiries arrive as long emails with half the facts missing, and each missing detail delays the proposal.

  • Wealth management

    Referrals deserve a personal reply, and review meetings slip when nobody has time to arrange them.

  • Health insurance

    Members want straight answers about cover, and HR managers want a quote brief handled without chasing.

  • Life sciences

    Requests arrive from many countries and events, and each needs to reach the right rep quickly.

  • Education

    Applicants and parents ask many small questions, and a slow reply can send them elsewhere.

  • Legal

    Intake takes fee-earner time, and a conflict check cannot start until the right names are in hand.

  • Car and van dealers

    Buyers message several dealers about the same car, and the first one to offer a test drive slot often wins the visit.

  • Home services and installers

    A quote needs photos and property details, and chasing them by phone wastes surveyors' days.

What stays the same in every industry

  • Written hand-off rulesYour team writes when a person takes over and can change the rules at any time. Agents never negotiate, discount or commit on your behalf unless you allow it.
  • The CRM stays trueEvery touch, score and outcome is written back to the CRM. A rep's manual edit always wins over an agent's change.
  • A pilot on your own conversationsAgents take a share of your real conversations and are measured against your current process before any wider rollout.
