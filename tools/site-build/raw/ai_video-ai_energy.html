What the cameras watch for on site

Hazardous sites need rules agreed with the people who run them. We settle what counts as an event before building.

  • PPE in hazardous areas

    Hard hats, hi-vis, gloves and other equipment checked where required, with a still frame sent to the person who can act.

  • Exclusion and permit zones

    People entering marked areas, or working in a zone without a permit on record, flagged to the control room.

  • Gauge and status reading

    Dials, indicator lights and equipment status read between rounds and logged, with unusual readings passed to an operator.

  • Visible leaks and spills

    Staining, pooling or visible vapour that a camera can see, raised for an operator to check in person.

  • Unmanned and remote sites

    Gate, fence and compound views checked for people or vehicles outside planned visits, with a still frame for the on-call contact.

  • Vehicle and lifting movements

    Vehicles in restricted routes or loads moving near people, logged with a short clip for review.

Rules your team sets and changes

You decide what the agent looks for, who is told and how quickly. Rules are written down before launch and reviewed with your safety team.

  • Zones and the permits they needPersonYour site team defines the zones and what counts as a valid permit. The agent only compares what you give it.
  • Normal ranges for readingsPersonEngineers set the range for each gauge. Readings outside it go to an operator, not to an automatic response.
  • Who is told, and whenPersonRouting by area, shift and severity, with a separate on-call route for unmanned sites.
  • No control of plantPersonThe agent raises alerts only. It does not operate valves, switches or safety systems.
  • What a person must reviewPersonEvery alert about people or equipment is reviewed by a named person before any follow-up.

Privacy and people on plant and remote sites

Operators, contractors and visitors are all on camera. These points are agreed with the site before a camera goes live.

  • On your estateProcessing runs on site servers or in your private cloud, so raw video is not sent to applyd.ai.
  • Limited connectivityRemote sites can process on the spot and send only short alerts and stills, rather than streaming everything.
  • Contractors and visitorsSignage and induction briefings tell contractors and visitors that cameras are in use and why.
  • Blurring, retention and assessmentBlurring and retention limits are set per camera, and we help with a data protection impact assessment and consultation.
