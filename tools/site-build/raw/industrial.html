The outlet stays steady. The effort to maintain it doesn't.

A heat exchanger holds its process outlet temperature, so that reading looks normal. But cooling flow is increasing and the pressure-drop trend is changing. Viewed together at comparable operating conditions, these signals can reveal declining performance, flagged as possible fouling for maintenance review.

Shared equipment. Relevant insight.

Pumps, compressors, heat exchangers and cooling systems appear in different industrial settings. Equipment behaviour and available sensor data shape the analysis, rather than the industry label alone.

  • Process pumpsMonitor vibration, electrical behaviour, pressure and flow to identify patterns associated with developing mechanical faults or abnormal pumping conditions.
  • CompressorsAnalyse mechanical and process measurements to identify changing equipment behaviour and signs of developing performance issues.
  • Heat exchangersTrack thermal performance, flow and pressure changes to identify patterns that may indicate fouling or declining heat-transfer efficiency.
  • Cooling and condenser fansMonitor vibration and bearing behaviour to identify developing mechanical issues in equipment supporting plant cooling.

Monitoring can extend to instrumented control valves, steam traps and other assets where suitable diagnostic data is available.
