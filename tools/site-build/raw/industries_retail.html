Order support and product questions for online shops

An AI agent for retail and e-commerce teams. It answers where-is-my-order and returns questions and product queries on WhatsApp, chat and Instagram, while refunds outside policy stay with your team.

WhatsApp, chat and Instagram where shoppers ask

  • WhatsApp messagesShoppers ask about an order or a product. The agent checks the order system and answers in your approved wording.
  • Website live chatPeople browsing ask about sizing, stock or delivery. The agent answers from your catalogue and nudges them towards checkout.
  • Instagram direct messagesQuestions about a product in a post or story get a reply with the right link and the stock position.
  • Email order queriesOrder and returns emails get a reply with tracking or a returns label, and anything unusual goes to a person.
  • Product page enquiriesQuestions asked on a product page are answered in the thread and logged, so you can see what shoppers need to know.

  • Start a return within policyEligible returns get a label and instructions. Anything outside your policy goes to a person with the order attached.
  • Answer product questions from the catalogueSizing, materials, compatibility and stock come from your catalogue, so answers match what you sell.

A where-is-my-order message, from tracking to a second sale

What shop owners and customer service leads ask about order support

  • Can an AI agent answer where-is-my-order messages?

    Yes. It checks your order system and replies with the tracking status and delivery date in your approved wording. If the courier reports a problem, or the order is missing, the conversation passes to a person with the order attached, so the shopper does not explain it twice.

  • How does it handle returns?

    Returns within your policy get a label and instructions straight away. Anything outside it, such as an item past the return window, passes to a person with the order attached. The agent never promises a refund or an exception, and your team decides every case outside policy.

  • Can it answer product questions and help sell?

    Yes. It answers questions about sizing, materials, compatibility and stock from your catalogue, and can suggest items from your range with links. It only offers prices and codes you have approved, so a product question can become a sale without extra work from your team.

  • Which channels does it work on?

    WhatsApp, website live chat, Instagram direct messages and email, with each thread kept in one history. Shoppers can switch channel without repeating themselves. You choose which channels go live first, and the agent follows the rules you set for each.

  • What happens with damaged or faulty items?

    The agent collects the order number and photos, then passes the case to a person. It does not decide whether the item is replaced or refunded. Your team makes that call, and the shopper sees a clear message about what happens next.
