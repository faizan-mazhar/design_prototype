A trial that went quiet, from nudge to demo booked

What heads of sales and growth leads ask about demos and trials

Can an AI agent handle demo requests for a SaaS team?

Yes. It replies to each request within minutes, asks a few fit questions in your wording and books a slot with the right rep. The rep sees the conversation and the fit result before the call, so the demo can start from what the buyer needs.

How does it follow up trial users?

It notices who has signed up but not logged in or reached a key step, and sends a short, useful message in your approved wording. If the user replies with a buying question, the agent passes them to a rep with the trial history attached.

Will the agent talk about pricing?

It shares only the published pricing you give it. Discounts, custom plans and negotiation pass to a rep with the conversation attached. The agent never invents a price or offers a discount, so a buyer asking for one reaches a person quickly.

What about security questionnaires and legal questions?

Those pass to the right person on your team. The agent does not complete questionnaires or make compliance claims on your behalf. Your security and legal leads decide what is said, and the buyer sees a clear message that the right person will reply.

How does it decide who is a good fit?

You set the fit rules, such as team size, use case and timeline, and the agent asks the questions and scores the answers. Strong fits go straight to a rep. Weaker ones get helpful material instead, and you can change the rules whenever you like.
