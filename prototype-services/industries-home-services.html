What installer owners and surveyors ask about job details and photos

Can the agent collect photos for a quote?

Yes. It asks homeowners for the specific photos your surveyor uses, such as the roof, boiler, meter or cylinder, over WhatsApp or email. It checks each one shows what is needed and asks again if not. Your surveyor then decides what the job needs.

Does it book site surveys?

Yes. It books from the surveyor's calendar by area, with a confirmation, access notes and a reminder. Homeowners can move the slot themselves. A surveyor sees the details and photos before the visit, so trips are not wasted on missing information.

Will the agent give a price?

No. Agents do not quote prices, give estimates or promise grants. They can explain how your quotes work and what happens at the survey. Pricing comes from your team once the details and photos are in, with the full conversation attached.

What happens when a homeowner asks about finance?

The agent notes the interest and passes it to authorised staff with the history. It does not discuss affordability, rates or approval, and it never says whether someone will be accepted. Your compliance team decides the wording for general questions, and we make no claim about regulatory status.

How does it deal with safety problems?

Your rules can flag gas smells, leaks, sparks and similar faults straight to a named person. The agent gives only the safety message you approved and stops the quote conversation. The person sees everything said so far and decides what to do next.
