What transport managers and sales desks ask about freight quotes

Can an AI agent handle freight quote requests?

Yes. It reads the request, writes the lane, weight, pallet count, dimensions and dates to one record and asks for any gaps. Your pricing desk receives a complete brief it can cost straight away, instead of a loose email that needs three follow-ups first.

Can it answer shipment status questions?

Yes. It checks your tracking or transport system and replies with the current status and expected delivery date. Routine questions are answered without your desk, while delays, damage or missing loads pass to a person with the shipment details attached.

Will the agent quote rates?

No. Rates, surcharges and contract pricing stay with your pricing team. The agent only gathers the details a quote needs and passes them on, so your team prices complete requests. It never estimates a price or promises a figure on your behalf.

How does it deal with special loads?

Dangerous goods, oversize loads, temperature control and urgent same-day jobs are flagged and passed to a person straight away. The agent does not judge whether a load can be carried. Your planners decide, and they see the full request and any details already captured.

Which channels does it cover?

Email and web quote forms first, because that is where freight requests usually arrive. Phone, WhatsApp and portal messages can follow. Every thread joins one record for the customer, so a status question links to the quote and the booking behind it.
