What are applyd.ai AI agents, and where does a person step in?

They are software agents that handle inbound conversations, sales and service: enquiries, bookings, orders, claims and renewals. They answer, qualify, follow up by email, SMS, WhatsApp and voice, and book meetings. Written hand-off rules decide when a person takes over, and the CRM is updated throughout.

All questions
Does this replace our SDR team?

No. The agents handle first responses and routine follow-up. Your reps get qualified conversations with a written brief, and they take over at the points you choose.

Is this only for sales leads?

No. The same agents handle sales enquiries and the service work around them: bookings, order questions, claims and renewals. The same five stages apply, and the same written hand-off rules decide when a person takes over, on every channel.

Can we control what the agents say?

Yes. You approve the message library, list topics the agents must hand to a person, and choose which steps need approval before anything is sent.

What happens when a lead asks about pricing?

The agent does not quote or negotiate. Pricing and contract questions are routed to the account owner with the conversation so far.

Do agents overwrite CRM edits?

No. A rep's manual edit always wins. If an agent's change conflicts with one, it flags the conflict and never overwrites the rep's change.
