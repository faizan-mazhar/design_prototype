Patient booking and reminders for clinics

An AI agent for clinics and practices. It books and reschedules appointments, sends reminders to reduce no-shows and fills cancelled slots, while clinical questions go to your clinicians.

How patients ask for an appointment

  • 01

    Phone calls to the front desk

    Callers book, move or cancel an appointment and hear confirmed options. Missed calls become a record with a short summary.

  • 02

    Online booking requests

    Requests from your website are checked against the diary and confirmed, or offered the nearest free slot.

  • 03

    SMS and WhatsApp reminders

    Patients get a reminder in your approved wording and can confirm, move or cancel with one reply.

  • 04

    Recall lists

    Patients due a check-up are invited to book, and each reply is written back to the record.

  • 05

    Email

    Booking questions and forms arrive in one history, so the team sees what was already said.

Fill the diary, remind patients, leave care to clinicians

  • 01

    Book into the real diary

    Slots come from the right clinician's calendar and appointment type, so the front desk is not untangling double bookings.

  • 02

    Remind and confirm

    A reminder goes out before each visit, and patients can confirm, move or cancel by reply. That is how the agent helps cut no-shows.

  • 03

    Refill cancelled slots

    When someone cancels, the agent offers the slot to patients on your waiting list and updates the diary.

  • 04

    Collect the practical details

    Agents gather what the visit needs, such as the appointment type and contact preferences, and leave symptoms and history to clinicians.

Rules for clinical questions, urgent symptoms and patient data

Examples of rules a team in this field might write. You set the real ones, and can change them at any time.

  • Person

    Clinical advice and symptoms

    Passed to a clinician. The agent does not diagnose, suggest treatment or say whether a symptom is serious.

  • Person

    Urgent or emergency wording

    Flagged to a named person at once, with the patient shown your approved emergency guidance. Your clinical lead writes the triggers.

  • Person

    Medication, results and referrals

    Handed to the clinical team. The agent does not discuss test results or change a prescription.

  • Approval

    Patient identity checks

    Details are confirmed in the way your practice requires before any personal information is shared.

  • Agent

    Booking, rescheduling and reminders

    Done on the diary within your appointment rules, with every change logged.

What practice managers ask about patient booking

Can an AI agent book patient appointments?

Yes. The agent books, moves and cancels appointments against your real diary, using the appointment types and clinician rules you set. Patients get a confirmation straight away, and anything outside those rules goes to your front desk with the conversation attached.

How does it help with no-shows?

It sends reminders before each visit and lets patients confirm, move or cancel with a reply. Freed slots can be offered to people on your waiting list. We make no claim about how much it will cut no-shows, because that depends on your practice.

Does the agent give medical advice?

No. It does not diagnose, suggest treatment or discuss results. Any clinical question, symptom or request about medication is passed to a clinician with the history attached. Your clinical lead decides which words and topics trigger that handover and what the agent may say.

What happens with an urgent message?

Your clinical lead writes the triggers, such as chest pain or severe bleeding. When one appears, the agent stops the booking flow, shows your approved emergency guidance and alerts a named person at once. It never judges urgency on its own.

How is patient information handled?

The agent collects only what booking needs and checks identity the way your practice requires before sharing anything. You decide what is stored, and your data protection lead should review the setup. Our security overview explains how data is handled. We make no claim of any certification.
