Quote intake and claim reporting for insurers

An AI agent for insurers and brokers. It takes quote details by phone or web form and logs first notice of loss out of hours, so your teams start each case with the facts. Decisions on cover and claims stay with people.

How customers reach you for a quote or a claim

  • 01

    Phone, including out of hours

    Callers report a loss or ask for a quote at any hour. The agent takes the details and leaves a full record for the morning.

  • 02

    Web quote forms

    Half-finished forms are followed up, and complete ones go to the right underwriter or broker.

  • 03

    Email and photos

    Customers send damage photos and documents, which are attached to the claim or quote record.

  • 04

    WhatsApp and chat

    Customers can report a loss or check progress in a thread that joins one history.

  • 05

    Broker and partner referrals

    Referrals carry their source, so you can see which partners bring the business you want.

Capture the details, start the claim or quote

  • 01

    Log first notice of loss

    The agent asks what happened, when and where, who was involved and whether anyone is hurt, then writes a structured record.

  • 02

    Take quote details

    It collects the facts a quote needs, such as property, vehicle or business details, and asks again for anything missing.

  • 03

    Point to safe next steps

    In approved wording, the agent tells claimants whom to call for emergency help and what to photograph, and leaves cover questions out.

  • 04

    Route to a handler

    Claims go to the right claims team and quotes to the right underwriter, with a summary and attachments ready.

Rules for cover opinions, emergencies and settlements

Examples of rules a team in this field might write. You set the real ones, and can change them at any time.

  • Person

    Cover opinions and claim outcomes

    Passed to a handler. The agent does not say whether a claim will be paid or what a policy covers.

  • Person

    Injury, emergency or danger

    Callers are pointed to emergency services in your approved wording, and a named person is alerted at once.

  • Approval

    Settlement offers and fault

    Left to your claims team. The agent does not discuss liability, blame or amounts.

  • Person

    Suspected fraud signals

    Flagged quietly to your team to review. The agent does not accuse anyone or change its tone.

  • Agent

    Quote intake and loss reports

    Details are taken in approved wording and written to the record, with attachments.

What claims and underwriting teams ask before a pilot

Can an AI agent take first notice of loss?

Yes. It asks what happened, when and where, who was involved and whether anyone is hurt, then writes a structured record with attachments. Claims teams start the morning with the facts instead of a voicemail. Cover and settlement decisions stay with your handlers.

Does it work out of hours?

Yes. Callers and web visitors get a prompt reply at any hour. The agent logs the claim or quote, points people to emergency help in your approved wording and leaves a clear record for your team to act on when they start work.

Will the agent tell customers if they are covered?

No. It does not give opinions on cover, fault or whether a claim will be paid. Those questions go to a handler with the conversation attached. Your compliance lead decides what the agent may say about policies and the claims process.

What happens if someone is in danger?

Your rules can tell the agent to point people to emergency services in your approved wording and alert a named person straight away. The agent does not try to handle the emergency itself and does not delay by asking claim questions first.

Can it help with quotes as well as claims?

Yes. It collects the facts a quote needs, follows up half-finished forms and routes complete requests to the right underwriter or broker. Pricing, underwriting and any regulated advice stay with your qualified people. Your team sets which quote types the agent may take, and everything it collects is logged on the record for review.
