Member answers and scheme quotes for health insurers

An AI agent for health insurers and brokers. It answers member questions from approved wording and turns employer scheme requests into complete quote briefs, while cover decisions stay with your team.

Where members and employers get in touch

  • 01

    Web quote request forms

    Employers and brokers state their headcount and cover wishes. The agent checks the form is complete and asks for what is missing.

  • 02

    Email from brokers and HR managers

    Scheme requests and renewals are read, sorted and turned into a record with the key facts pulled out.

  • 03

    Member portal and chat questions

    Members ask about their plan and get answers from wording you approved, with a link to the right document.

  • 04

    Phone and voicemail

    Missed calls become records with a short summary, so a person can call back knowing the question.

  • 05

    Broker referrals

    Referrals carry their source, so you can see which brokers send the schemes you want.

Answer from approved wording, brief the quote team

  • 01

    Complete the quote brief

    The agent collects headcount, locations, current insurer, renewal date and cover wishes, so a quote handler starts with a full picture.

  • 02

    Answer plan questions

    Members get answers about waiting periods, how to claim and where to find documents, using only the wording you approved.

  • 03

    Route to the right desk

    Scheme quotes go to sales, claims questions to claims and complaints to the right person, each with the history attached.

  • 04

    Chase missing information

    When a scheme request lacks a census file or renewal date, the agent asks the contact again and logs the reply.

Rules for cover decisions, claims and health information

Examples of rules a team in this field might write. You set the real ones, and can change them at any time.

  • Person

    Cover and eligibility decisions

    Passed to your team. The agent does not say whether a treatment or condition is covered.

  • Person

    Claims and pre-authorisation

    Sent to the claims team with the history. The agent explains the process but does not judge an outcome.

  • Person

    Medical details and pre-existing conditions

    Not discussed in chat. Anyone describing health details is moved to a trained person.

  • Approval

    Pricing and underwriting

    Only published or approved figures are shared. Anything bespoke is left to an underwriter.

  • Agent

    Quote intake and member FAQs

    Handled in approved wording, with each answer logged against the member or scheme.

An employer scheme request, from web form to quote brief

What distribution and member teams ask before a pilot

Can an AI agent answer member questions for a health insurer?

Yes, from wording you approve. It explains how to claim, where to find documents and what a plan term means in plain words. Anything about whether a specific treatment is covered goes to your team with the conversation attached. Your compliance lead decides which topics the agent may explain.

How does it handle employer scheme requests?

It reads web forms and broker emails, pulls out headcount, locations, renewal date and cover wishes, and asks for anything missing. Your quote team then starts from a complete brief instead of chasing the basics. Pricing stays with your underwriters.

Does the agent decide what is covered?

No. It does not say whether a condition, treatment or claim is covered or approved. Those questions pass to a trained person with the history attached. Your compliance lead decides which topics the agent may explain and which it must hand over.

What if someone shares medical details?

Your rules can stop the agent from discussing health details and move the person to a trained colleague straight away. The agent collects only what the request needs and asks people not to send detailed medical information by chat. Your compliance lead sets the exact triggers.

Can it work with brokers?

Yes. Broker emails and referrals are read, logged with their source and turned into complete briefs. You can see which brokers send which kind of scheme. Commercial terms and broker relationships stay with your distribution team. You decide which brokers the agent may reply to directly and which go straight to a person, and every message is logged.
