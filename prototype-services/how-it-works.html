Five agents, one job each

Five steps for every conversation, sales or service: capture, qualify, follow up, book and sync. Every stage has a written rule for when a person takes over.

Agents run on the platform your team controls. Read the security overview or book a demo to see it on your funnel.

Scroll
01 / 05

Capture every inbound lead

Picks up leads from forms, site chat, shared inboxes and ad lead forms. Removes duplicates and enriches the record before anyone looks at it.

Hands to a person when the lead belongs to an open opportunity. It goes straight to that owner.

Listens to
Web forms, site chat, shared inboxes, ad lead forms
Produces
One clean record with source attribution
02 / 05

Qualify against your own rules

Scores fit and intent using the criteria your team already applies, and asks the follow-up questions a good SDR would ask. Every score comes with its reasons in plain language.

Hands to a person when the score crosses your threshold, or the lead asks to speak to someone.

Uses
Your ICP definition, territory rules, past win data
Produces
A score with the reasons written out
03 / 05

Follow up in your voice

Runs sequences across email, SMS and WhatsApp from your approved message library. Stops as soon as someone replies and responds to what they actually said.

Hands to a person when a reply raises pricing, contract terms or anything outside approved answers.

Channels
Email, SMS, WhatsApp, voice (you choose which)
Guardrails
Approved messages, send windows, opt-out handling
04 / 05

Book the right rep

Finds a time that suits the prospect and the assigned rep, sends the invite, handles reschedules and writes a short brief for the call.

Hands to a person when the account needs a named owner or an executive sponsor.

Connects to
Team calendars and routing rules
Produces
A confirmed meeting and a call brief
05 / 05

Keep the CRM true

Writes every touch, score and outcome back to your CRM as it happens, so forecasts don't depend on reps typing notes at the end of the day.

Hands to a person when a field conflicts with a manual edit. It flags the conflict and never overwrites a rep's change.

Writes
Activities, stages, scores, notes, owner changes
Produces
An audit trail for every automated action

Your team decides where the agents stop

You set the hand-off rules before launch and can change them at any time. Agents never negotiate, discount or commit on your behalf unless you allow it.

  • Person

    Pricing and contract questions

    Routed to the account owner with the conversation so far.

  • Person

    High-fit accounts

    Above your score threshold, a rep takes the first call.

  • Person approves

    New message templates

    Nothing new is sent until a manager approves it.

  • Agent

    Routine follow-up and scheduling

    Handled end to end and logged in the CRM.

How does an AI agent hand a lead over to a sales rep?

Each of the five agents has a written rule for when it stops. When a lead meets a rule, such as asking about pricing or crossing your score threshold, the agent passes the conversation to the right rep with the full history and a short brief, and the CRM record shows who took over.

How a pilot goes live

Agents earn their share of your leads. Each step ends with something your team can check before the next one starts.

  • Prepare

    Nothing changes for your reps yet

    1. Connect systems
    2. Agree rules and hand-offs

  • Prove it before launch

    Tested on leads that already happened

    1. Evaluation set on your past leads
    2. Guardrails and approvals
    3. Security review

    Below target: adjust the rules and messages, then run the evaluation again.

  • Go live

    A share of real leads, compared with your team

    1. Staged rollout on a share of leads
    2. Monitor agent against team
    3. Widen or stop
  1. 01

    Connect systems

    Link your CRM, inboxes, calendars and lead sources with read access first, so the agents can see how leads move today.

    In practice
    Least-privilege access, read-only until the rules are agreed
    You get
    A map of where your leads come from and where they end up
  2. 02

    Agree rules and hand-offs

    Your team writes the qualification criteria, the approved messages and the exact moments a person takes over.

    In practice
    Written hand-off rules owned by your sales leadership
    You get
    A rulebook your reps and managers have signed off
  3. 03

    Evaluation set on your past leads

    Agents work through a sample of your past leads, and your team marks where they would have acted differently.

    In practice
    A held-out set of real past leads with the outcome your team wanted
    You get
    A score for qualification, follow-up and hand-off decisions
  4. 04

    Guardrails and approvals

    You choose which actions need a person to approve them, and set the limits on how much an agent may send.

    In practice
    Approval steps for risky actions, rate and send limits
    You get
    A list of what agents may do alone and what waits for a person
  5. 05

    Security review

    Your security team reviews the setup and the documents in our security pack, which we share under NDA.

    In practice
    Review against your own vendor process
    You get
    Written sign-off, or a list of changes to make first
  6. 06

    Staged rollout on a share of leads

    Agents take a share of new inbound leads. The rest stay with your team as the comparison.

    In practice
    Split by lead source or segment, with a way to switch agents off at any time
    You get
    Two groups of leads handled side by side
  7. 07

    Monitor agent against team

    Response time, qualification accuracy, meetings booked and hand-offs are tracked for the agent group and the team group.

    In practice
    Every action traced and reviewed by sampling
    You get
    A report your leadership can read without us
  8. 08

    Widen or stop

    Your team looks at the numbers and decides: widen the share, change the rules and keep testing, or stop.

    In practice
    A decision made by people, with the evidence in front of them
    You get
    A rollout decision you can defend
