What we build for chat

Most projects start with one channel and one audience. We agree the scope, the hand-over rules and the test set before building.

  • 01

    Customer service assistants

    Order, account and policy questions answered from your help content and systems, each with a link to its source.

  • 02

    Internal knowledge assistants

    Staff ask in plain language and get an answer from your policies and wikis, limited to what they may already open.

  • 03

    Hand-over to people

    The agent who takes over sees the transcript, the customer's details and what the assistant already tried.

  • 04

    Several channels

    A website widget, a messaging app, Teams, Slack or a chat screen in your own app, all sharing the same knowledge and rules.

  • 05

    Languages and voice

    Replies in the language the customer writes in, and speech in and out where phone calls matter more than chat.

  • 06

    Analytics and guardrails

    A view of what people asked and where the assistant handed over. Unanswered questions become your content backlog.

How we keep assistants safe

An assistant speaks for your business. These limits are set before launch.

  • Grounded

    Approved content only

    Answers come from sources you approve, and the assistant says when it does not know.

  • Person

    Hand-over rules you write

    Complaints, money, sensitive topics and direct requests go to a person with the whole conversation.

  • Checked

    Input and output guards

    Personal data is masked, injection attempts blocked and replies checked before they are sent.

  • Tested

    A test set of real questions

    Every release is run against questions your team actually gets.

Common questions about this work

Can the assistant hand over to a person?

Yes, and we treat it as a core feature. You set the triggers, such as low confidence, a named topic or a request for a person. The person gets the transcript and customer details, so the customer does not repeat themselves.

How do you stop it making things up?

It answers only from content you approve and is told to say it does not know rather than guess. Each answer links to its source, and every release runs against a test set of real questions.

Can it connect to our help desk and CRM?

Usually, through the APIs those systems provide. It can look up an order or ticket, create a case or update a contact within permissions we agree. Read-only access comes first.

Can it work in more than one language?

Yes. Quality varies between models and languages, so we test candidate models on your own questions in each language before choosing one.

What is the difference between a chatbot and an agent?

A chatbot holds a conversation: it answers, collects details and passes work to people. An agent carries out multi-step tasks on its own. Many projects start as a chatbot and add a few agent-style actions once answers are reliable.
