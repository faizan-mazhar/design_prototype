What we do before anything is built

Engagements range from a short readiness assessment to a pilot. We agree the questions to answer and the deliverables at the start.

  • 01

    Use-case selection

    A workshop-led review of where AI could help, then a ranked shortlist scored on value, feasibility, data and risk, with the reasons shown.

  • 02

    Data readiness

    Where the data lives, who owns it, how clean it is and whether you may use it for this purpose, with the gaps in priority order.

  • 03

    Build, buy or fine-tune

    For each shortlisted case, a comparison of a ready-made product, a custom build, retrieval over your documents and fine-tuning.

  • 04

    Proof of concept

    A small build on your real data, judged against a pass mark and a test set agreed in advance.

  • 05

    Cost modelling

    Projected running costs at your expected volumes, and how model choice changes them.

  • 06

    Governance and roadmap

    A view on data protection and AI regulation for each use case, and a phased plan from pilot to production.

    Common questions about this work

    What does an AI readiness assessment include?

    A review of candidate use cases, the data behind them, your systems and your governance position. You receive a scored shortlist, a data gap list, an outline cost model and a recommended next step for each case, as a written document.

    Should we fine-tune a model or use RAG?

    Usually RAG first. Retrieval ties answers to your current documents, is cheaper to change and makes citing sources easy. Fine-tuning helps with a consistent style, a narrow high-volume task or behaviour prompting cannot reach. When unclear, we test both on the same evaluation set.

    Does AI regulation apply to us?

    It can, depending on where your users are and what each system does. We map your use cases to their likely risk category and flag where you need formal legal advice. Data protection law applies whenever personal data is involved.

    Will you recommend that we hire you to build it?

    Only where a custom build is the right answer. If a ready-made product meets the need, the recommendation says so.

    What happens after the recommendation?

    You decide. You can take the plan in-house, put it out to tender or ask us to deliver the pilot. If we build it, the success criteria and evaluation plan carry straight into the scope of work.
