Viewing booking and buyer qualifying for estate agents

An AI agent for estate and letting agents. It answers portal and WhatsApp enquiries, checks proof of funds and move dates and books viewings, while offers and negotiation stay with your negotiators.

Rules for offers, negotiation and tenant referencing

Examples of rules a team in this field might write. You set the real ones, and can change them at any time.

  • Person

    Offers and negotiation

    Passed to a negotiator. The agent never receives, accepts or relays an offer, and does not comment on what a seller will take.

  • Person

    Tenant referencing and right-to-rent checks

    Done by your team. The agent only says that checks are needed and does not judge whether someone will pass.

  • Person

    Mortgage and financial advice

    The agent notes what the buyer says about funds but does not advise on mortgages or affordability.

  • Approval

    Fees, deposits and contract terms

    Only terms you have approved are shared, and anything bespoke is left to a person.

  • Agent

    Qualifying questions and viewing booking

    Asked in approved wording and booked on the negotiator's calendar, which saves viewings on people who cannot proceed.

A buyer enquiry, from evening message to viewing

What agency principals and lettings managers ask about viewings

Can an AI agent book viewings for an estate agent?

Yes. It replies to portal and WhatsApp enquiries, confirms the property is available and offers slots from the negotiator's calendar. The booking includes a confirmation, a meeting point and a reminder. The negotiator sees the conversation before the visit, so nothing needs repeating.

How does it qualify buyers and tenants?

It asks the questions you approve, such as whether a buyer has a property to sell, a mortgage in principle or proof of funds, and when they hope to move. For tenants it asks about move date, household and budget. It records the answers and does not judge them.

Will the agent handle offers or negotiation?

No. Offers, price discussions and anything about what a seller will accept pass to a negotiator straight away, with the history attached. The agent does not receive, relay or comment on offers. Your team decides who contacts the buyer and when.

What about referencing and right-to-rent checks?

Those stay with your team. The agent can say that checks are part of the process and collect the contact details you need to start them. It does not decide whether someone passes, and it does not give legal or financial advice.

What happens to enquiries that arrive out of hours?

They are answered straight away, in your approved wording, and offered viewing slots for the next working day. Anything that needs a person is flagged for the morning, so negotiators start with the conversation already summarised rather than a cold list of names.
