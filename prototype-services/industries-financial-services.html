Client onboarding and document chasing for financial firms

An AI agent for financial firms. It guides new clients through onboarding, chases missing ID and statements, and tells your team when a file is complete. Regulated advice stays with your advisers.

How clients send documents and answers

  • 01

    Email with secure upload links

    Clients reply to a plain email and send documents through a secure upload link, so nothing sensitive sits in an inbox.

  • 02

    Client portal

    The agent checks what has arrived against your checklist and tells the client what is still missing.

  • 03

    Website onboarding forms

    New clients start a form online and the agent follows up if they stop part-way.

  • 04

    WhatsApp or SMS nudges

    A short reminder with a link prompts clients to finish, without sending documents over the message itself.

  • 05

    Phone and voicemail

    Missed calls become records with a summary, so your team can call back knowing the file status.

Guide onboarding, chase what is missing

  • 01

    Explain what is needed

    Clients get a clear checklist in your approved wording, such as proof of identity, proof of address and recent statements.

  • 02

    Chase missing documents

    The agent follows up on a schedule you set, and stops when the item arrives or a person takes over.

  • 03

    Check the file for completeness

    Each document is matched to the checklist, and the file shows what is received and what is outstanding. Your team does the actual checks.

  • 04

    Hand over a complete file

    When the checklist is done, the case goes to an adviser or onboarding officer with every document and message attached.

Rules for regulated advice, identity checks and sensitive data

Examples of rules a team in this field might write. You set the real ones, and can change them at any time.

  • Person

    Investment, tax and product advice

    Passed to a qualified adviser. The agent does not recommend products or say what a client should do.

  • Approval

    Identity and anti-money-laundering decisions

    Left to your compliance team. The agent collects documents but does not judge whether they pass.

  • Person

    Vulnerable or distressed clients

    Passed to a person at once and handled under your own policy.

  • Person

    Complaints

    Sent to your complaints process with the history attached, with no argument from the agent.

  • Agent

    Checklist updates and reminders

    Sent within the schedule and wording you approved, and logged on the file.

What compliance and operations teams ask about onboarding

Can an AI agent help with client onboarding?

Yes. It sends a checklist, answers practical questions about what is needed, chases missing items and tells your team when the file is complete. The checks, approvals and any advice stay with your onboarding and compliance staff. You write the checklist and the wording, and can change either at any time.

How does it collect documents safely?

Clients use a secure upload link or your portal, so documents are not left in email threads. The agent matches each file to your checklist and records what has arrived. You decide where files are stored, and your data protection lead should review the setup.

Does the agent give financial advice?

No. It does not recommend products or say what a client should do. Any request for advice passes to a qualified adviser with the history attached. Your compliance lead decides what the agent may say, in line with your own regulatory obligations.

Does it decide whether identity checks pass?

No. The agent collects documents and shows whether the checklist is complete. Whether an identity or source-of-funds check passes is a judgement for your compliance team, who see the file and make the call under your own procedures. The agent never marks a check as passed.

How often does it chase clients?

You set the schedule and the wording. A common pattern is a reminder after a few days, then a gentler follow-up, then a handover to a person. The agent stops as soon as the item arrives, and clients can ask to speak to someone.
