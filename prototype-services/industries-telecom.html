A cancellation request, from chat to a retained customer

What contact centre leads and retention managers ask about coverage and cancellations

Can an AI agent run coverage checks?

Yes. It looks up your coverage data by postcode and replies in approved wording, including what is not yet available. If a customer needs a site survey or a complex quote, the agent passes them to a person with the address and what they asked for.

How does it help keep customers who want to leave?

It spots cancel and switch requests as soon as they arrive, asks one question about the reason and hands the customer to a retention person with the history. It does not offer deals itself, so the first minute is used well and offers stay with your team.

Will the agent offer discounts or retention deals?

No. Only your retention team makes offers. The agent does not offer, hint at or negotiate a discount, and it does not say what a customer owes to leave. Your team decides every offer, and the agent passes the reason and plan so they can make it.

What about vulnerable customers and complaints?

Your rules can pass those conversations to a named person immediately. The agent stops replying on the topic, and the person sees the conversation so far. Your compliance team decides what counts as vulnerable under your own policy, and we make no claim about regulatory status.

Which channels does it work on?

Live chat and phone first, with WhatsApp, SMS, email and web forms alongside. Each customer has one history across them, so a chat that moves to a call does not start again. You choose which channels go live and what the agent may say on each.
