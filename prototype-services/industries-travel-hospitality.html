What sales managers and event teams ask about group requests

Can an AI agent handle group booking requests?

Yes. It reads the enquiry, writes the dates, headcount, rooms, meeting space and budget to one record and asks short questions about any gaps. Your sales manager then receives a clear brief and a draft outline, ready to price and send as a proposal.

Will the agent quote rates or agree terms?

No. Rates, discounts, package prices, deposits and cancellation terms stay with your sales team. The agent can explain how your proposals work and what happens next, and it never confirms a booking. Your manager prices the proposal using the brief the agent prepared.

How does it check availability?

It reads held dates and room blocks from your booking system or diary and uses them to tell your team what is free. If the diary changes, the record follows. The agent does not hold dates or promise space unless your rules say it may.

What about special requests and accessibility needs?

The agent records dietary, accessibility and other needs exactly as the sender wrote them and passes them to the right team. It does not promise that a request can be met. Your event team confirms what is possible and replies directly.

Which channels does it cover?

Email and web enquiry forms first, because that is where group requests usually arrive. Phone, WhatsApp and live chat can follow, with one record behind every thread. You decide which channels go live and what the agent may say on each.
