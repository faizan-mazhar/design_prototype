Client intake and conflict-check details for law firms

An AI agent for law firms. It takes client intake from web enquiries and email, gathers the details your conflict checks need and books consultations, while legal advice and sensitive matters stay with your solicitors.

Where enquiries arrive, from web forms to email

  • 01

    Web enquiry forms

    The agent acknowledges each enquiry, asks the intake questions you approved and passes the details to the right team.

  • 02

    Email

    Enquiries by email are read, turned into a matter record and answered in approved wording, with attachments filed.

  • 03

    Referrals from other professionals

    Introductions carry their source, so the firm can see which relationships bring in work.

  • 04

    Out-of-hours messages

    People often write in the evening. They get a prompt reply and a consultation slot to pick.

  • 05

    Phone and voicemail

    Missed calls become records with a short summary, so the team can call back with context.

Take intake, collect the names, stop before advice

  • 01

    Take the intake in approved wording

    The agent asks who is involved, the area of law and the key dates, and keeps case facts short. Detail is left for the solicitor.

  • 02

    Gather names for conflict checks

    It collects the names of the people and organisations involved, so your conflict check can start before the first call.

  • 03

    Route to the right team

    Enquiries are sorted by practice area, such as property, employment or family, and sent to the right fee earner.

  • 04

    Book a consultation

    Slots come from the fee earner's calendar, with a confirmation in approved wording and the history attached.

Rules for advice, sensitive matters and conflicts

Examples of rules a team in this field might write. You set the real ones, and can change them at any time.

  • Person

    Legal advice and case assessment

    Passed to a solicitor. The agent does not say whether someone has a case, what their rights are or what will happen next.

  • Person

    Urgent or time-limited matters

    Flagged to a named person at once, such as an arrest, a court deadline or a limitation concern, so nothing waits in a queue.

  • Person

    Safeguarding, domestic abuse and distress

    Passed to a person immediately and handled under your own policy. The agent does not carry on the conversation.

  • Approval

    Fees, quotes and engagement terms

    Only fee information you have approved is shared, and anything bespoke is left to a solicitor.

  • Agent

    Intake questions and consultation booking

    Asked in approved wording and booked on the fee earner's calendar, which saves time on basic details.

What partners and practice managers ask about client intake

Can an AI agent handle client intake for a law firm?

Yes, for intake. Agents reply to enquiries, ask the questions you approved, gather the names your conflict check needs and book a consultation with the right fee earner. They do not give legal advice, so assessments and next steps pass to your solicitors.

Does the agent give legal advice?

No. Agents do not say whether someone has a case, explain their rights or predict outcomes. Your team writes what counts as advice, and those conversations pass to a solicitor with the history attached. Your compliance officer decides the wording the agent may use, in line with your SRA obligations.

Can it help with conflict checks?

It can collect the names of the parties and organisations involved and write them to the record, so your team can run the check before the first call. The agent does not decide whether a conflict exists. That judgement and any decision to accept the matter stay with your people.

What about urgent or sensitive matters?

Your rules can pass urgent or time-limited matters, safeguarding concerns and any sign of distress straight to a named person. The agent stops replying on that topic and the person sees the conversation so far. Your team can add other triggers that match your own policies.

How does it treat client confidentiality?

The agent collects only what intake needs and asks people not to send detailed case facts by chat. You decide what is stored, and your data protection lead should review the setup. Our security overview explains how data is handled. We make no claim of any certification.
