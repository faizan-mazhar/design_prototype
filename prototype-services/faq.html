Every question in one place

Does this replace our SDR team?

No. The agents handle first responses and routine follow-up. Your reps get qualified conversations with a written brief, and they take over at the points you choose.

Is this only for sales leads?

No. The same agents handle sales enquiries and the service work around them: bookings, order questions, claims and renewals. The same five stages apply, and the same written hand-off rules decide when a person takes over, on every channel.

Can we control what the agents say?

Yes. You approve the message library, list topics the agents must hand to a person, and choose which steps need approval before anything is sent.

What happens when a lead asks about pricing?

The agent does not quote or negotiate. Pricing and contract questions are routed to the account owner with the conversation so far.

Do agents overwrite CRM edits?

No. A rep's manual edit always wins. If an agent's change conflicts with one, it flags the conflict and never overwrites the rep's change.

Which channels do the agents work on?

Web forms, site chat, email, SMS, WhatsApp and voice. Every channel lands in one conversation history per lead, so a rep never asks the same question twice.

How is a pilot measured?

Agents take a share of your leads and are compared with your current process on time to first response, qualification rate, meetings held and CRM completeness.

Can we approve messages before they're sent?

Yes. Nothing new is sent until a manager has approved it, and you choose which steps need approval.

Where are hand-off rules set?

You set them with your team before launch as written rules, and you can change them at any time. Admins control who can edit playbooks and hand-off rules.

Where can our security team find details?

Start with the security overview, which covers tenant isolation, access and audit, and how agents are kept in bounds. Hosting, model data use, subprocessors and certifications are in the security pack, which we share under NDA.
